# Escalation Management Business Process

## Tujuan

Mengelola proses escalation ticket ketika SLA hampir habis atau telah terlewati.

---

## Actor

### IT Supervisor

- Monitoring Escalation
- Menangani Escalation
- Melakukan Reassignment

### IT Manager

- Monitoring Critical Escalation

### IT Staff

- Menindaklanjuti Ticket Escalation

---

## Escalation Level

### Level 1

Trigger:
80% SLA

Target:
Supervisor

### Level 2

Trigger:
100% SLA

Target:
Manager

### Level 3

Trigger:
120% SLA

Target:
Executive Management

---

## Escalation Flow

1. Ticket dibuat

2. SLA Timer berjalan

3. Sistem menghitung persentase SLA

4. Mencapai 80% SLA

5. Sistem membuat Escalation Level 1

6. Mencapai 100% SLA

7. Sistem membuat Escalation Level 2

8. Mencapai 120% SLA

9. Sistem membuat Escalation Level 3

10. Supervisor atau Manager melakukan tindakan

---

## Output

- Escalation History tersedia
- Escalation Monitoring tersedia
- Notification tersedia
- Escalation Report tersedia
