# Ticket Management Business Process

## Tujuan

Mengelola seluruh proses permintaan layanan dan gangguan IT dari pengguna sampai selesai.

---

## Actor

### Employee

- Membuat Ticket
- Melihat Status Ticket
- Memberikan Komentar
- Memberikan Rating

### IT Staff

- Mengerjakan Ticket
- Mengubah Status Ticket
- Memberikan Solusi

### IT Supervisor

- Melakukan Assignment
- Monitoring SLA
- Monitoring Escalation

### IT Manager

- Monitoring KPI
- Monitoring Department Performance

---

## Ticket Workflow

NEW

↓

ASSIGNED

↓

IN PROGRESS

↓

PENDING

↓

RESOLVED

↓

CLOSED

---

## Ticket Creation Flow

1. User Login

2. User Memilih Service

3. User Mengisi Form Ticket

4. Sistem Membuat Ticket

5. Sistem Menghitung SLA

6. Supervisor Melakukan Assignment

7. IT Staff Mengerjakan Ticket

8. Ticket Diselesaikan

9. User Memberikan Rating

10. Ticket Ditutup

---

## Output

- Ticket Number tersedia
- Ticket History tersedia
- Ticket Assignment tersedia
- Ticket SLA tersedia
- Ticket Report tersedia
