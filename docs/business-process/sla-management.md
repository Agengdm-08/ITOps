# SLA Management Business Process

## Tujuan

Mengelola target waktu pelayanan ticket berdasarkan tingkat prioritas yang telah ditentukan perusahaan.

---

## Actor

### IT Supervisor

- Membuat SLA Policy
- Mengatur SLA Rule
- Monitoring SLA

### IT Manager

- Menentukan SLA Target
- Monitoring SLA Achievement

### IT Staff

- Menyelesaikan Ticket sesuai SLA

---

## SLA Priority Matrix

| Priority | Response Time | Resolution Time |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Low | 4 Jam | 24 Jam |
| Medium | 2 Jam | 8 Jam |
| High | 1 Jam | 4 Jam |
| Critical | 15 Menit | 1 Jam |

---

## SLA Flow

1. User membuat ticket

2. Sistem menentukan priority

3. Sistem mengambil SLA Rule

4. Sistem menghitung Response Deadline

5. Sistem menghitung Resolution Deadline

6. SLA Timer berjalan

7. IT Staff mengerjakan ticket

8. Sistem memonitor SLA

9. Ticket diselesaikan

10. Sistem menghitung SLA Achievement

---

## Output

- SLA Policy tersedia
- SLA Rule tersedia
- SLA Timer tersedia
- Response Deadline tersedia
- Resolution Deadline tersedia
- SLA Achievement tersedia
- SLA Report tersedia
- SLA Breach Report tersedia
